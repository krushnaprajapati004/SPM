Guidance for using FarmPro.

Back to home
How do I add a record?

Open a section such as Crops, Labor, Inventory or Expenses, complete the form and press Add Record. Saved records are kept in this browser.

How do I use search?

Use the top search box to find matching content on this page, or the records search field to filter the table.

How do I switch dark and light mode?

Use the moon/sun button in the top bar or Appearance in Settings.

How do I log out?

Select Log out from the sidebar. The sign-in screen will appear.
